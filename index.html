IPO watch

How applying works

Every IPO in India is applied for through your broker or bank, using UPI or ASBA.

Pick an IPO

Compare price band, lot size, subscription and dates. Retail investors can bid up to ₹2 lakh on mainboard issues.

Apply in your app

Tap Apply, choose your broker or bank and place the bid there. Bidding at cut-off price gives the best chance.

Approve the UPI mandate

Accept the mandate in your UPI app before 4 PM on the closing day. The money is blocked, not debited.

Track it here

Use “I've applied, track it” to follow allotment, refund and listing in Applied.
